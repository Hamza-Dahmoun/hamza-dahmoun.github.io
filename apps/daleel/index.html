Remember a few words of a verse or a hadith but not where it is? Type them in Arabic and Daleel finds it, in the Quran or in nine major Hadith collections. Everyday spelling matches the Uthmani Quran script, so “رب العالمين” finds “رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ”.

  • Exact phrase search across the Quran (Hafs and Warsh) and 62,000+ hadiths
  • Bookmarks, copy and share
  • Works fully offline — no account, no ads, no tracking
