05 // apps

Mobile apps.

Daleel: Quran & Hadith Finder دليل القرآن والأحاديث

Android, in closed testing on Google Play · iOS from the same codebase

An offline search engine for Arabic religious texts. You type the words you remember, and Daleel finds the matching verse or hadith across the full Quran (Hafs and Warsh) and 62,000+ hadiths from nine collections.

The UI and search logic are written once in Kotlin and shared between Android and iOS. A custom Arabic normalizer ignores diacritics, hamza variants and Uthmani spelling, so everyday typing still matches. The content database is built at compile time and bundled with the app: no network, no data collected.

RattibMe رتّبني

Android, coming soon to Google Play · built with Flutter, so iOS can follow from the same code

A sliding-number puzzle game for Android, built with Flutter and Dart. Players put the tiles in order across 114 levels, on boards from 3×3 to 8×8, with fixed numbered cells and rocks added along the way.

A built-in solver checks that every level can be solved and powers the hints and the “Solve it for me” button. The seasonal level road, its landmarks and the tiles are all drawn in code, with no licensed artwork. The game plays offline in 11 languages, including right-to-left Arabic, and is funded by Google AdMob ads with Google’s consent form.
